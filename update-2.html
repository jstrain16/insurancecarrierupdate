The Breakdown

What changed this week

APPETITE & CONTEST · E&S CASUALTY / PROPERTY
Hanover — Excess & Surplus
  • Writing GL, Property & Excess. Excess can't be stand-alone — they still need the underlying GL. Direct bill on all accounts, and E&S can team up with Small Commercial / Mid-Market to put all lines on one bill.
  • Open classes: solar panel installers, roofers (commercial & residential), custom home builders, tree trimmers, demolition (no wrecking balls/explosives), food manufacturing, bars & taverns up to 75% alcohol sales (GL & Property, no XS), metal manufacturing, LROs & vacant buildings (up to $20M/location, $35M/policy), historic buildings 100+ years old, mini-golf & bowling alleys, wholesalers/distributors. Colorado is carved out on new residential contractors & custom home builders.
  • Eff Jun 8 – Jul 15 $20 Amazon gift card for every new E&S account issued directly with Hanover during the contest window.
Do this: If you've got an E&S account on your desk, send it to surplus@hanover.com for clearance, then get Josh your comparisons.
APPETITE · NEW RESIDENTIAL CONSTRUCTION
Hanover E&S — Residential Construction Appetite
  • Target: trade & general contractors specializing in custom home construction; 5–30 employees; gross sales $1M–$15M; max home value $5M; work at/below 5 stories; 5+ years in business preferred.
  • UT, MT & WY are broad-appetite states — min premium starts at $9,500 ($12,500 in limited states like CA, FL, NJ, NV, OR, TX, WA). Custom/spec single-family capped at 25 units per project (10 in limited states); multi-family up to 25 units broad / out of appetite in limited states.
  • Not in appetite: door/window, foundation, EFIS/plaster/stucco, waterproofing (as direct trade work); states AK, AR, CO, HI, NY, SC, WV.
Do this: Custom home builders in Utah, Montana or Wyoming that fit the box are a great Hanover fit — quote them and send Josh the comparison.
TERRITORY · MULTI-LINE
Selective
  • Eff Jul 1 Selective will be writing Montana & Wyoming. New states added to their footprint, confirmed by Matthew Fish.
Do this: If you've got business up in MT or WY, start running it through Selective at renewal. Send new business to SubHubWest@selective.com and cc Matthew.
